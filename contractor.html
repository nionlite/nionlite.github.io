Not a generic design toy

A proposal engine for contractors.

Generic AI design apps show pretty ideas. Contractor is built around the business workflow: capture the job, estimate the work, package the options, publish a branded proposal, get approval, track deposit, send an invoice, and close the project.

Contractor-first workflow

Every screen is aimed at helping a contractor leave the site with a clear proposal path: customer, project, photos, measurements, notes, package options, terms, approval, and follow-up.

Trade-specific modules

Each module can define its own fields, job types, materials, formulas, AI prompts, design styles, proposal sections, and pricing assumptions.

Human control before publishing

AI can draft ideas and estimates, but the contractor reviews and controls measurements, costs, labor, markup, scope, taxes, deposits, and exclusions before anything is sent.

Module-based architecture

One app, many contractor niches.

Complete MVP workflow

From lead to closed project, without leaving the app.

The MVP is focused on the sales-to-closeout workflow a solo contractor or small contractor team needs before heavier systems like accounting integrations, crew scheduling, inventory, and marketplace features.

Create customer and project

Add customer details, site address, project name, module, job type, business branding, and internal notes.

Capture photos and measurements

Take or upload site photos, then enter trade-specific dimensions manually so the contractor remains in control of quantities and assumptions.

Generate draft ideas and estimates

Use AI-assisted design ideas and estimate drafts as a starting point. Adjust materials, labor, markup, taxes, deposit terms, exclusions, and package differences before publishing.

Build Good / Better / Best packages

Turn one job into clear customer choices with different scopes, materials, upgrades, pricing, and deposit amounts.

Publish and share proposal

Create a customer-ready proposal summary, share it through the device, and keep a published snapshot so accepted scope and prices stay preserved.

Approval, deposit, invoice, closeout

Record customer approval, manually confirm deposit, create and send invoice records, manually confirm payment, and move the project to closed status.

What the app is and is not

Built for proposal control, not blind automation.

The product deliberately avoids claiming that AI can guarantee job costs. Contractor helps draft, structure, and present work, while the contractor remains responsible for the final business decision.

Drafting ideas: AI-assisted concepts, scope language, and design direction by module.Professional judgment: Final design, code compliance, structural decisions, permits, and customer commitments.
Estimating workflow: Material quantities, labor assumptions, markup, tax, deposit, package totals, and editable line items.Guaranteed pricing: Contractors must verify costs, measurements, local rates, taxes, site constraints, and supplier pricing.

Frequently asked questions

Questions before review.

Is Contractor only for landscaping?

No. Landscaping is the first module, but the app is built as a modular contractor platform. Painting, outdoor living, kitchen, bathroom, and room design modules can each have their own fields, formulas, prompts, and templates.

Does AI create guaranteed estimates?

No. AI helps draft ideas and estimate starting points. Contractors must verify measurements, pricing, material choices, labor assumptions, taxes, deposits, and proposal terms before sending anything to a customer.

Does the MVP process real customer payments?

No. Customer deposit and invoice payment confirmation are manual in the MVP. The contractor records payment status after confirming it outside the app.

Where is project data stored?

The first release is local-first. Data is stored on the device, with backup export and restore options so contractors can save backup files to destinations they control.
