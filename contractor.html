The project workflow

From site visit to project closeout.

Set up the job in the field, prepare a proposal for customer review, and keep the invoice and payment history tied to the accepted scope.

Create customer and project

Add customer details, site address, project name, module, job type, business branding, and internal notes.

Capture photos and measurements

Take or upload site photos, then enter trade-specific dimensions manually so the contractor remains in control of quantities and assumptions.

Generate draft ideas and estimates

Draft written design directions from local templates and calculate estimates from your measurements and rates. Adjust materials, labor, markup, taxes, deposit terms, exclusions, and package differences before publishing.

Build Good / Better / Best packages

Turn one job into clear customer choices with different scopes, materials, upgrades, pricing, and deposit amounts.

Publish and share proposal

Create a branded proposal PDF and share it through the iOS share sheet. Published versions preserve the scope and prices the customer reviewed.

Approval, deposit, invoice, closeout

Record customer approval, manually confirm deposit, create and send invoice records, manually confirm payment, and move the project to closed status.

Frequently asked questions

Common questions about Contractor.

Which trades are supported?

Landscaping, decks, patios and porches, painting, kitchen remodeling, bathroom remodeling, and room design each have their own job types and estimating inputs.

How are design ideas and estimates created?

Written design directions come from templates on your device. Estimates use your measurements and rates. Site photos serve as project references, and the contractor reviews all scope and prices before sharing.

How do I record approvals and payments?

Share the proposal PDF with your customer, then record the chosen package and approval in the project. Record deposits and invoice payments once you receive them through your usual payment method.

Can I see a proposal before subscribing?

Yes. Open Settings and tap Sample proposal to preview a customer-ready PDF. Settings also includes FAQs and subscription details.

What happens if my trial or subscription ends?

Your existing projects and published PDFs stay available to view and share. You can still create and restore backup files. Active Pro access is needed to create or edit project work.

Where is project data stored?

Your workspace lives on your device. In Settings, export a backup file to Files, iCloud Drive, or Google Drive. Choose Restore from backup file to review and import it later.
