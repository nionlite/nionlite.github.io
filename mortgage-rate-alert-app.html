Mortgage Rate Alerts

Mortgage rate alerts for published credit union and bank rates.

CUB: Track daily Mortgage rate includes Premium rate drop alerts so users can watch selected lenders and target rates. Alerts are designed for borrowers who want to know when a published rate moves closer to their goal.

Direct answer: CUB can be used as a mortgage rate alert app for published credit union and bank rates. Users can save lenders, set target alerts, and monitor movement without manually rechecking every lender page.

Why alerts matter

  • Mortgage rates can change quickly during active shopping windows.
  • Credit union rates may not appear in broad lender marketplaces.
  • A small rate movement can matter on a large loan amount.
  • Alerts help users watch a shortlist instead of the entire market.

What to verify before applying

Always confirm the rate, APR, fees, points, credit assumptions, down payment, property type, geography, and membership requirements directly with the lender.

A rate alert is not a rate lock

A CUB alert reports movement in publicly posted lender information. It does not reserve a rate, confirm borrower eligibility, or create a binding offer. A rate lock is an agreement with a lender for a stated period and can depend on the details of an active application.

How to choose a useful target

Start with a realistic borrowing scenario rather than a headline number. Decide which loan type and term you are watching, whether points are acceptable, and which lenders you could actually join or use. A target is more useful when it is attached to a shortlist and a plan for what you will verify next.

  • Watch the same mortgage product across comparable lenders.
  • Check whether the published rate includes discount points.
  • Confirm state availability and credit union membership.
  • Ask the lender for current rate, APR, fees, and lock terms.
  • Compare formal Loan Estimates before making a decision.

When alerts are most useful

Alerts can help during the research period before an application, while building a lender shortlist, or when watching a small group of institutions over time. Once you are under contract or close to a deadline, communicate directly with lenders because a public rate may change before the app detects it or may not apply to your scenario.
