Compare credit union mortgage rates by state availability.

Credit union mortgage rates are only useful if the borrower can realistically qualify for the lender. CUB helps users narrow published rates with state selection, lender details, membership context, and favorites.

Direct answer: Premium in CUB includes state selection and advanced filters, helping users compare credit union and bank mortgage rates by where a lender is available and what membership path may apply.

What state filtering helps solve

Mortgage shoppers often find attractive credit union rates that are not realistic because of geography or membership requirements. State filtering helps reduce noise before users spend time contacting lenders.

Useful filters in CUB

Premium users can filter by bank, credit union, military, employer, regional, local, donation-based paths, state availability, and favorites.

State availability and membership are different

A lender may operate in your state without being open to every resident, and membership in a credit union does not guarantee that every mortgage product is available for every property or loan amount. Treat a state filter as the first narrowing step, then verify the institution's field of membership and mortgage coverage.

How to compare a state-level shortlist

  1. Select the state where the property is located.
  2. Choose the lender and membership categories that fit your situation.
  3. Open lender details and verify the current membership path directly.
  4. Compare the same mortgage product, term, points, and lock assumptions.
  5. Save realistic lenders and request comparable Loan Estimates.

Do not stop at the lowest published rate

Review APR, points, lender credits, origination charges, mortgage insurance, cash to close, and rate-lock terms. The lowest visible interest rate can carry more upfront cost or assumptions that do not match your application.
